NEAR Against Its Peers is a reproducible investment thesis on NEAR. It compares NEAR's market cap per dollar of on-chain fees with 5 peer chains, decides which NEAR Intents revenue can fairly count for NEAR, and states in advance the results that would prove it wrong. Counting the Intents revenue its ecosystem captures, NEAR is valued at 0.33 times the peer median, below its typical peer; like for like, on base-layer fees alone, it is valued at 3.07 times, higher than every peer. On the captured-revenue reading, the Intents revenue behind it held up over the last window, but holders do not yet provably capture that revenue. The investment case rests on the treasury burning or locking what it buys back; until then the strict holder reading is BASE. Multiples: 441x counting Intents revenue, 4,072x on base-layer fees, against a peer median of 1,326x. Falsification criteria, thresholds frozen in a tagged commit: C1, C2 and C3 hold; C4 fires. A Python standard-library pipeline turns a committed DefiLlama and CoinGecko snapshot (as of 12 Nov 2026) into metrics.json, THESIS.md and a static dashboard; make judge-demo rebuilds everything offline and runs the tests, and every number and verdict in the text is generated from the data.
