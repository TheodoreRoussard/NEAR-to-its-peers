## The question

For each dollar of fees paid on-chain, does the market value NEAR more or less than its peers? And which perimeter that includes NEAR Intents can be defended?

The measure is a **multiple**: market cap divided by the fees summed over the trailing {{trailing_days}} days. A lower multiple means the market pays less for each dollar of activity. Fees are the top line, before the cost of token issuance, so this is a price-to-sales ratio, not a price-to-earnings ratio. Subtracting issuance would need data this repository does not compute.

- **BASE+NET is an assumption about the treasury.** It counts the Intents revenue the NEAR ecosystem keeps as if it worked for NEAR holders. That is the defended perimeter, stated as an assumption, not a fact.
- **The comparable Intents history is short.** Revenue has been booked daily only since {{regime_break}}: {{regime_days}} days of data.
- **Fees reflect pricing as well as activity.** NEAR prices its gas low by design, so a busier NEAR can still collect few base-layer fees. A chain that cuts its prices raises its multiple without becoming less useful.

## Limitations

- **Not computed.** The data in this repository cannot support these, so the thesis does not claim them:
  - fully diluted valuations, which locked supply makes non-comparable across chains;
  - a reverse sum-of-the-parts of NEAR's market cap;
  - the application revenue of the peers (the symmetric rule that would give every chain credit for apps that buy back or burn its token);
  - the split of Intents growth between volume and take rate;
  - NEAR's own front-end fees, separated from protocol fees.
- **Ethereum's fees exclude its rollups**, which understates the activity Ethereum anchors.
- **Intents looks more like a cross-chain exchange than a blockchain.** A second panel of cross-chain venues would test BASE+NET from another angle.
- **Supply growth covers {{float_period}}.** CoinGecko's free API serves about a year of history. Market cap divided by price is a proxy for circulating supply: it includes unlocks and CoinGecko's own supply revisions.
- **One snapshot.** Every figure is dated {{as_of}}. `make all` refreshes the data; the verdicts above are recomputed, never typed.
- **DefiLlama attribution.** Fee definitions follow DefiLlama's adapters, linked below, and change when those adapters change.
