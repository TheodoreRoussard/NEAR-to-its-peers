# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Hackathon judges for the NEAR Hackathon Investment Thesis track: professional crypto investors, plus the Claude Code and Codex screeners that check the repository first. They read on desktop during the judging window (16–18 Nov 2026) and judge "the evidence, not the conviction". Confirmed by the author on 2026-11-13.

## Product Purpose
A reproducible investment thesis on NEAR. It compares NEAR's market cap per dollar of on-chain fees with peer chains, decides which NEAR Intents revenue can fairly count, and states in advance the results that would prove it wrong. Success: a judge understands the claim, its two readings and its weak points within a minute, and can trace every number to its source.

## Positioning
Every number and every verdict is generated from one committed data snapshot, and the falsification thresholds were frozen in a tagged commit. The thesis argues against itself in the open: the like-for-like reading, which is unfavourable, is stated next to the favourable one.

## Operating Context
Public GitHub repository, `make judge-demo` offline rebuild, `THESIS.md`, and a static dashboard (`docs/index.html`) published on GitHub Pages and opened by judges from the submission form and an X post.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML and CSS only, generated by `src/render.py`. No scripts and no external assets: it must work offline and over `file://`.
- No hand-typed numbers: every number comes from `data/processed/metrics.json`.
- Light and dark themes; no horizontal page overflow at phone width.
- Banned wording: "cheapest", "returned to the token".
- Content may be trimmed or merged in the dashboard as long as every number stays sourced (author, 2026-11-13).

## Brand Commitments
- Not financial advice, stated on every surface.
- Data credits: "Data from DefiLlama" and "Powered by CoinGecko", displayed prominently.
- The author does not want a SaaS dashboard look (cards, pills, badges, rounded boxes) or crypto-brand gloss (neon, gradients, glowing dark mode). Confirmed 2026-11-13.

## Evidence on Hand
`data/raw/` snapshot (DefiLlama, CoinGecko), `config/panel.toml` (panel rule, frozen thresholds), and the on-chain buyback figures recorded as sourced constants. No testimonials, endorsements or partner logos exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
- The claim is stated with its counter-reading, never alone.
- Every figure carries its source and method beside it.
- Verdicts are computed, never written.
